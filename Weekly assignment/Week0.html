Week 0

  • Learned about teamwork, responsibility, and the importance of collaboration.

  • Started building a sense of community with the batch and faculty mentors.

Reflection

Week 0 was more than an orientation. It gave everyone a chance to introduce themselves, understand their goals, and begin forming a strong collaborative mindset for the work ahead.
