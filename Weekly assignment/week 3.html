Week 3

Electronics Fundamentals Review & Fusion 360 Practical Assignments

This week focused on strengthening the basics of electronics and digital systems while also applying practical design thinking in Fusion 360. The combination of theory and hands-on practice helped me connect core electrical concepts with mechanical design, simulation, and real-world problem solving.

  • Reviewed important electronics fundamentals such as voltage, current, resistance, and circuit behavior.
  • Explored how electronic components work together in practical systems and real-world devices.
  • Worked through Fusion 360 tasks to improve design accuracy, modeling skills, and technical confidence.
  • Connected design thinking with engineering fundamentals by applying theory to structured practical work.

Big takeaway

Strong engineering starts with understanding fundamentals. When theory, tools, and practice are combined effectively, even simple projects can become meaningful and technically valuable.

Core Learning Focus

The week began with a revision of essential electronics topics, helping us revisit how circuits are built and how current flows through components. These fundamentals are important because they form the foundation for many engineering systems, from microcontrollers to machine design and automation.

As the week progressed, the Fusion 360 practical tasks gave us the opportunity to apply structured thinking to design work. It was useful to see how technical knowledge and software tools support each other, especially when creating precise, realistic models for engineering projects.

Overall, Week 3 helped me understand the value of combining concept review with hands-on execution. It reinforced that good engineering depends not only on creativity, but also on clarity of understanding, careful analysis, and repeated practice.
