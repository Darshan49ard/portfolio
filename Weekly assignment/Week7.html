Week 7

IoT, Connectivity & Real-Time Systems

This week focused on the role of IoT in connecting devices, exchanging data, and enabling responsive systems. The session helped me understand how connectivity, sensor data, and communication protocols work together to make modern devices more intelligent and interactive.

  • Explored the basics of IoT and how connected devices communicate in real time.
  • Learned how sensors, data flow, and network connectivity support smart systems.
  • Understood the value of reliable communication and integration in automation and control.
  • Connected physical devices with digital workflows to improve responsiveness and efficiency.

Big takeaway

IoT is more than connected devices; it is about meaningful communication between systems. When data moves reliably, the overall solution becomes smarter, faster, and more useful.

Week 7 gave me a clearer view of how IoT blends hardware, communication, and intelligence. It helped me realize that real-time connectivity is essential for building systems that can respond quickly, monitor conditions, and create value in everyday automation and smart environments.
