Week 6

Figma, UI/UX Development & Design Practice

This week focused on using Figma to explore digital design and translate ideas into practical, user-friendly interfaces. The session helped connect creativity with usability, turning rough concepts into structured layout decisions and interaction-ready designs.

  • Worked with Figma to create and refine user interface ideas.
  • Explored how visual hierarchy, spacing, and layout affect usability.
  • Learned the importance of thinking from the user perspective during design decisions.
  • Practiced turning design concepts into interactive, cleaner digital experiences.

Big takeaway

Good UI/UX design is not only about appearance. It is about making the experience intuitive, efficient, and helpful for the user while keeping the design clear and engaging.

Week 6 helped me understand that design is a combination of creativity and structure. By using Figma and focusing on UI/UX principles, I gained a better understanding of how digital experiences are shaped and improved through thoughtful decisions, user awareness, and iteration.
