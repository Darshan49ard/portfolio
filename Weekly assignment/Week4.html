Week 4

Design Thinking & Problem Framing

This week focused on turning ideas into structured solutions by thinking about the actual problem, understanding user needs, and working together to frame practical concepts. The session helped me improve not only my creativity but also my ability to communicate and refine ideas clearly.

  • Discussed how to identify the right problem before jumping into a solution.
  • Explored creative thinking and brainstorming techniques for team-based idea generation.
  • Learned how observation, user perspective, and clarity of purpose shape better design decisions.
  • Practiced turning rough ideas into more meaningful and testable concepts.

Big takeaway

Strong ideas begin with strong understanding. When the problem is clearly framed, the solution becomes easier to design, explain, and improve.

Week 4 was important because it reminded me that good engineering and design are not only about technical execution, but also about understanding what problem is worth solving. By focusing on framing, discussion, and iteration, I was able to see how creative thinking can become a more structured and practical process.
