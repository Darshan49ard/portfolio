Week 0 – Orientation & Introduction                                          

  The Protosem officially kicked off in Week 0 with an orientation session that laid the groundwork for the entire semester. This session walked through how the program would run — from the weekly rhythm of tasks and assignments to how progress would be tracked and documented right up to Week 20. It was a helpful way to set expectations early and understand the level of consistency and effort the seminar would demand going forward.

A key part of the orientation was a self-assessment exercise, where I took the 16Personalities test. My result came back as the Campaigner (ENFP-T) — a personality type known for being imaginative, energetic, and people-oriented, with a natural inclination toward exploring new ideas and thinking outside conventional boundaries. Campaigners are typically described as enthusiastic problem-solvers who thrive on creativity and spontaneity rather than rigid routines, and who bring genuine curiosity to whatever they're working on. Going through this result was a good moment of self-reflection, and it resonated with how I usually approach hands-on, open-ended engineering work — favoring exploration, adaptability, and collaboration over strictly following a fixed process.

The week closed out with self-introductions to the rest of the batch and the mentors. This gave everyone a chance to share a bit of their background, interests, and goals for the semester. For my part, I spoke about my background in Mechatronics Engineering, my interest in robotics, automation, and AI, and what I was hoping to take away from the Protosem experience. It was also a good opportunity to get a first glimpse of the people I'd be collaborating with over the coming weeks — a small but important step in building the sense of team that the rest of the semester would build on.
